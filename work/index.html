Rebuilding a premium apparel store on Shopify 2.0

Migrated OROS, a technical outdoor apparel brand built around its SOLARCORE® insulation, from a Shopify 1.0 theme to Online Store 2.0. I rebuilt the product pages, collections, responsive layouts and custom store features in Liquid, JavaScript and CSS as reusable sections and blocks instead of third-party apps, keeping the brand's design intact. I used Claude to analyse the old theme code and speed up development and troubleshooting, and reviewed and tested every change myself. The result is a storefront that is easier to maintain and ready to grow.

Moving a family apparel brand to Shopify 2.0, with WordPress for content

Fore Fathers sells apparel for dads and families, from men's polos, hats and socks to Daddy & Me matching sets and bundles. I migrated their Shopify store from a 1.0 theme to Online Store 2.0 and customised the storefront with my own Liquid, JavaScript and CSS where that fit better than an app. WordPress stayed the home for the brand's blog and content, and Shopify became the store. I migrated the existing content as part of the move and mapped product links and redirects so existing URLs still lead to the right Shopify products and collections. AI-assisted tools were part of my workflow for code analysis and troubleshooting, and the implementation decisions were mine.

From Figma to a custom Shopify 2.0 store with a dynamic product page

Daytrip handed me a complete design in Figma, and I built it as a custom Shopify 2.0 theme instead of adapting an off-the-shelf one: the homepage, collection pages and a heavily customised product page, responsive across desktop, tablet and mobile. The product page was the main technical piece. Switching a variant doesn't just change the price. It also updates the background colour and section-specific content on the page. I built that with Shopify's Section Rendering API: when the variant changes, my JavaScript requests the affected sections rendered for that variant and swaps them in without a page reload, so everything still comes from Shopify's own Liquid templates. I also made the store ADA compliant for accessibility.

Connecting Gravity Forms to Advyzon and Wealthbox with a custom plugin

The client needed Gravity Forms submissions sent to two CRMs, Advyzon and Wealthbox, and neither Zapier nor any existing integration could handle what they needed. So I built a WordPress plugin from scratch. It picks up each submission, maps the form fields to the right fields in each CRM, and sends the data directly to the Advyzon and Wealthbox APIs. Each CRM has its own integration logic, and there's no third-party automation service in between.

Technical work on an online coffee store: speed, QuickBooks and SEO

Those Coffee People sells farm-direct Colombian coffee online. My work here was on the technical side, not the design. I optimized the site's loading speed and performance, and it now scores around 65 on mobile and 92 on desktop in Google PageSpeed. I connected the online store to QuickBooks so the store and the accounting system can talk to each other. I also cleaned up technical SEO issues across the site, fixing broken links, 404 errors and URLs that pointed to the wrong place.
