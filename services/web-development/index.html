FAQ

Web design questions, answered

How much does a website cost in Portugal?

It depends on the platform and the scope. A WordPress business site, a Shopify store and a custom React application are very different amounts of work, and so are five pages and fifty. I give a fixed quote once I know what the site needs to do, so you know the full cost before any work starts. Tell me about the project and I'll send you one.

How do you build a website for a business?

In four steps. I start by understanding what the site has to do for the business, then choose the platform and agree the scope. I build it with speed, security and mobile layouts in from the start, and after launch I stay reachable for fixes and changes.

Should my site be WordPress, Shopify or a custom React build?

WordPress suits content-led sites and businesses that want to edit their own pages. Shopify is usually the simplest way to run an online store. A custom React application makes sense when the site has to do something no platform does well, such as a booking flow or a client dashboard. I recommend the one that fits, even when it's the smaller job.

Do you work with UK and US businesses from Portugal?

Yes, online. Portugal is on the same time zone as the UK, so I work your hours and we can talk during your day. For businesses in the US, my afternoons overlap with the East Coast morning, which gives us a daily window for video calls.

Are your websites built with SEO in mind?

Yes. I work as an SEO web developer as well as a builder: clean page structure, fast loading, proper titles and headings, structured data and a sitemap are part of every build, not extras added later. Ongoing SEO and Google Business Profile work is covered on my marketing services page.
