AI & Automation

AI & Automation Services for Businesses in Porto

A lot of what a business does by hand every day, replying to enquiries, working out a quote, checking a website, follows the same steps every time. I build systems that do those steps automatically, so they happen in seconds instead of sitting in an inbox.

AI Chatbots & Automated Replies

Systems that read an incoming message, whether it's a contact-form submission, an email, or a chat widget, and reply automatically in a natural, human-sounding way, instead of it sitting unanswered until someone has time.

  • Reads and understands incoming form, email, or chat messages
  • Drafts a natural, on-topic reply, matched to the business's own tone
  • Sends the reply immediately, any hour, with no manual copy-pasting

Every enquiry gets an immediate, human-sounding response, without someone at a desk typing it.

Workflow & Business Process Automation

A lot of business tasks, working out a quote, applying the right rate, generating an invoice, follow the exact same steps every time. I build workflows that do those steps automatically, using the pricing and rules a business already works from.

  • Extracts the relevant details from an incoming form or email automatically
  • Looks up rates or pricing from an existing spreadsheet or system
  • Calculates totals, taxes, or other business logic automatically
  • Generates and sends the resulting document, a quote or an invoice, without manual work

A multi-step manual process turns into something that runs in the background, in minutes.

AI-Powered Reports & Audits

Tools that take an input, a website, a document, a dataset, analyze it automatically, and use AI to turn that analysis into a clear report someone can actually read and act on.

  • Collects an input, like a website or document, through a simple form
  • Analyzes the content or structure automatically on the backend
  • Uses AI to turn that raw analysis into a clear, readable report
  • Delivers the finished report back automatically, by email or on-screen

Turns raw data into something a person can actually read and act on, without anyone writing it by hand.

API Integrations & Connected Systems

None of the above works if the tools a business already uses don't talk to each other. I connect forms, inboxes, spreadsheets, and other software through their APIs, so information moves between them without anyone copying it by hand.

  • API-based integrations between forms, email, spreadsheets, and other business tools
  • Keeping data in sync automatically between systems that don't connect out of the box
  • Custom middleware when two tools need to work together but weren't built to
