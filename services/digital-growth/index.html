Digital Growth

Digital Growth Services for Businesses in Porto

I help businesses improve their visibility on Google, understand their website traffic, and turn more visitors into enquiries and customers.

Google Business Profile

For most local businesses, your Google Business Profile is seen more than your website. I optimize it properly: categories, business information, services, photos, and the review setup, so it works for you.

  • Profile optimization and accurate business information
  • Correct categories and service listings
  • Photos that represent the business well
  • A working process for collecting reviews

Help local customers find your business when they search on Google and Google Maps.

Local SEO

Local SEO is a combination of technical foundations, on-page content, and relevance signals that tell Google your business genuinely serves a given area. I work on all three, not just one.

  • Technical SEO foundations (site speed, crawlability, indexing)
  • On-page SEO and local relevance
  • Internal linking and structured data (schema.org)
  • Content that matches what your customers actually search for

The goal is durable search visibility. I won't promise a guaranteed #1 ranking, because no one honestly can.

Google Ads

Search campaigns built around the keywords people actually use when they're ready to buy or enquire, with conversion tracking in place from day one so you know what a click is actually worth.

  • Search campaign setup and keyword targeting
  • Conversion tracking configured before launch
  • Landing pages built to convert that traffic
  • Ongoing performance measurement, not "set and forget"

The focus is qualified traffic that's likely to convert, not just a higher click count.

Meta Ads

Facebook and Instagram campaigns built around a specific audience and a landing page designed for that audience, with conversion tracking so results are actually measurable.

  • Audience targeting on Facebook and Instagram
  • Creative testing to see what actually resonates
  • Landing pages matched to the campaign
  • Conversion tracking across the funnel

Analytics & Tracking

You should know which channels and actions are actually generating enquiries, not guess. I set up Google Analytics 4 and Google Tag Manager properly, with event and conversion tracking that reflects what actually matters to your business.

  • Google Analytics 4 setup and configuration
  • Google Tag Manager for clean, maintainable tracking
  • Event and conversion tracking tied to real business actions

Conversion Optimization

Getting more traffic matters less if the site doesn't convert it. I look at clarity of CTAs, the landing page and user journey, forms, mobile experience, and trust signals, then test changes rather than guessing.

  • Clearer calls to action and landing pages
  • Simplified user journeys and forms
  • Mobile experience and trust signals
  • Testing changes instead of guessing

The focus is converting the traffic you already have, not just chasing more of it.
