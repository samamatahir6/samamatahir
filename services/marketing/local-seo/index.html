How It Ranks

How local search results are decided

Google says local results come down to three things: relevance (does the business match the search), distance (how close it is to the person searching) and prominence (how well known and well reviewed it is). You can't move your address, so local SEO works on the other two.

Relevance comes from the right categories, complete services and pages on your site that describe what you do and where. Prominence comes from reviews, consistent listings in directories, links from local sites, and a website Google trusts. I work on both, and I keep the website and the profile saying the same thing.

FAQ

Questions about local SEO services

What do local SEO services include?

Google Business Profile optimisation, citation cleanup, service and location pages on your website, a review routine, technical fixes on the site, and tracking of calls and enquiries from local search.

How long does local SEO take?

Profile and citation fixes can show up within a few weeks. Reviews, pages and authority build over months. Distance to the person searching also matters, and that part cannot be changed.

Do I need a website for local SEO?

You can rank in the map results with a profile alone, but a website with clear service and location pages makes the profile stronger and gives people somewhere to go before they call.

Can you do local SEO for more than one location?

Yes. Each location needs its own profile, citations and page, with consistent details, so the work scales per location.

How much do local SEO services cost?

It depends on how competitive your area and services are, and how much needs fixing. After a first look at your profile and site, I send a clear quote.

Can I do local SEO myself?

You can do a lot of it. Keep your Google Business Profile complete, ask every happy customer for a review, and make sure your details match everywhere. I help when you want it done faster, or when the problem is on the website side.
