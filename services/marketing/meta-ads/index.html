When Meta Ads Work

Meta Ads management for leads and sales

Facebook and Instagram ads are good at reaching people who are not searching yet: they show your offer to an audience likely to want it. That makes them a good fit for products with a visual hook, local services with a clear offer, and remarketing to people who visited your site but did not buy.

They are a weaker fit when people only buy after searching for a specific need. Then Google Ads usually works better, and I will tell you so.

Tracking First

Why the pixel and Conversions API come first

Meta's ad system learns from the conversions you send it. If the pixel fires on the wrong page, counts a purchase twice, or misses most events because of browser blocking, the algorithm optimises for the wrong people and the reports can't be trusted. Before I spend a euro on ads, I check the pixel, set up the Conversions API so events are also sent from the server, and confirm that the numbers in Events Manager match the sales or leads you actually got.

On Shopify and WordPress this is usually quick. On custom sites it takes a little developer work, which I can do myself.

Creative

What makes Facebook and Instagram ads work

Most of the result comes from the offer and the creative, not from clever targeting. I help you shape a clear offer, write the copy and plan simple image and video ads you can make with what you have. Then we test a few versions, keep what works and stop what doesn't.

FAQ

Facebook and Instagram ads questions

What are Meta Ads?

Meta Ads are the paid ads that run on Facebook, Instagram, Messenger and Meta's partner apps, all managed from Meta Ads Manager.

How do Meta Ads work?

You choose a goal, an audience and a budget, and Meta shows your ads to the people in that audience most likely to take the action you chose. The better your tracking, the better Meta gets at finding those people.

How much do Instagram ads cost?

Your ad budget is paid to Meta directly, and the cost per result depends on your audience, offer and creative. I help you set a test budget and adjust it on real results. My management is quoted separately.

What is the Conversions API?

It sends conversion events from your website's server to Meta, alongside the browser pixel. That keeps your results measurable when browsers or ad blockers stop the pixel from firing.

Should I run Facebook ads or Google Ads?

Google Ads reach people who are already searching for what you offer. Facebook and Instagram ads reach people who are not searching yet. Which works better depends on the product and how people buy it, and many businesses use both.

Do I need a big budget for Meta Ads?

No, but the budget has to be enough for the campaign to learn. I suggest a starting budget after looking at your offer and what a customer is worth to you, and I tell you honestly if ads don't make sense yet.

Can you run Instagram ads only?

Yes. Instagram and Facebook ads are run from the same Meta Ads Manager, so I can limit a campaign to Instagram placements. I usually test both first, because the cheaper results are not always where you expect them.

Want Facebook and Instagram ads that you can measure?

Tell me what you sell and who buys it. I will tell you honestly whether Meta Ads are the right channel.
