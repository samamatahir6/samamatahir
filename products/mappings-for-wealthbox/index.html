Why I Built It

Your leads live on your website. Your business lives in Wealthbox.

Gravity Forms is great at catching leads, but Wealthbox is where advisors actually work. Getting one into the other usually means paying for an automation service or copying entries by hand.

I built Mappings for Wealthbox to close that gap right inside WordPress. Pick a form, match the fields, and every new lead lands in Wealthbox on its own. Pro adds custom fields, contact updates, sync logs and nested forms.

How It Works

Three steps, no code

  1. 1

    Connect

    Paste your Wealthbox API token under Wealthbox, Settings, then click Test connection.

  2. 2

    Map

    Pick a Gravity Form. Choose the Wealthbox field for each form field from a searchable list.

  3. 3

    Publish

    Every new submission now creates the contact in Wealthbox.

  4. 4

    Check (Pro)

    Returning people are updated, not duplicated, and every sync is in the log.

See It Work

Inside your WordPress dashboard

Three screens do the work: the mapping screen, the contact matching and the sync log.

The Flow

From submit button to CRM in seconds

Everything runs inside your own WordPress site. No third party service sits in the middle.

  1. Form submitted

    A visitor fills in any Gravity Form.

  2. Mapping applied

    Each field goes to its Wealthbox field.

  3. Contact matched

    Pro checks email or phone for a returning person.

  4. Saved in Wealthbox

    Created new, or updated in place.

  5. Logged

    Pro records the result for you to check.

FAQ

Straight answers

Do I need Zapier or another automation tool?

No. The plugin talks to the Wealthbox API directly from your WordPress site.

Will it update people who are already in my Wealthbox?

Pro updates contacts the plugin has already created or updated. Matching contacts that were in Wealthbox before you installed it is coming in an update.

Does it send my old form entries?

No. Only submissions made after you publish a mapping are sent.

Where is my data stored?

In your own WordPress database and in Wealthbox. Nothing passes through my servers. Pro keeps sync logs in your database and uses fingerprints, not raw emails, for matching.

How many sites does one Pro licence cover?

One site per licence. Buy one for each site you run Pro on.

Is this plugin made by Wealthbox?

No. It is an independent plugin built by Samama Tahir. It is not made or endorsed by Wealthbox.
